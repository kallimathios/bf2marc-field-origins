Inside the 008: one field, three sources

Character positions of the exported 008. The converter picks the Books (BK) layout because the Work is bf:Text and the Instance issuance is not serial or integrating.

What the export has that the conversion didn't produce

  • 001 is the local HRID in00000437360. The converter writes 23117934 from the Instance adminMetadata bf:identifiedBy/bf:Local.
  • 005 is a local timestamp. The converter stamps the conversion time.
  • 008/38 is o in the export; the converter leaves it blank.
  • The second 020 (ISBN-10 6240017884), both 035 OCLC numbers, 948, 994, and the Stanford 999 aren't in either BIBFRAME description.
  • 040 $d UtOrBLW replaces the converter's $d DLC $d DLC-MRC $d DLC chain.

What the conversion produced that the export dropped

  • 003 DLC, plus the LC local fields 906, 925, 955, and LC's 999. All come from bflc:marcKey values in the Instance adminMetadata.
  • $0 URIs on 043, 334, 336, 337, and 338.

How this was traced

The v2.10 rules were compiled with src/compile.xsl and run (libxslt) on the Instance with its linked Work, as the stylesheet expects. Each top-level property of the Work and Instance was then removed one at a time and the record re-converted; the fields that disappeared or changed are the ones attributed to that property. Results were checked against the context XPaths in rules/*.xml.
